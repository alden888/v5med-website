Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from medical distributors before their first order.

What certifications do V5 Medical consumables carry?

Our manufacturing partners operate under ISO 13485:2016 quality management systems. Products carry CE marking under EU MDR, and we provide full technical documentation packages (including sterilization validation and test reports) to support FDA registration and local market approvals.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ varies by product category. We support flexible MOQs for pilot and trial orders so new distributors can verify quality before scaling. Contact our sales team with your target SKU list for an exact quotation.

Do you offer OEM/ODM private labeling?

Yes. We provide comprehensive OEM branding (custom packaging, labels, IFU) and ODM product development for established distributors, including regulatory documentation issued under your brand where applicable.

Which markets do you ship to and what are the lead times?

We ship worldwide from Shanghai/Ningbo ports, with established distributor networks in Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Typical lead time is 3–7 days for samples and 15–30 days for production orders, depending on sterilization and customization requirements.
