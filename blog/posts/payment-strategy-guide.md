# Payment Strategy: The "Two-Lane" System for Faster Delivery

> **Time is the most expensive currency in medical procurement.**
> Waiting 3-5 days for a bank wire (T/T) to clear just to ship a $50 sample box is a bottleneck we have officially removed.

At **V5 Medical**, we operate on a "Efficiency First" philosophy. To support our partners in 2026, we have restructured our financial workflows into two distinct lanes: the **Fast Lane (Credit Card)** and the **Commercial Lane (T/T)**.

This guide explains how to use them effectively.

---

## 🏎️ Lane 1: The "Fast Lane" for Samples
**Best for:** Samples, Evaluation Kits, Small Trial Orders, Consulting Fees.
**Limit:** Under $500 USD.

In the past, getting a sample shipped involved exchanging invoices, going to the bank, and waiting for SWIFT confirmation. Now, it's instant.

## 🚢 Lane 2: The "Commercial Lane" for Bulk
**Best for:** Mass Production, Container Shipments, Stocking Orders.
**Requirement:** Invoice Value > $500 USD.

For commercial orders, we strictly adhere to global trade compliance.

### 🏦 Why T/T (Bank Wire) for Bulk?
You might ask: *"Why can't I pay $50,000 via Credit Card?"*

1.  **Cost Control:** Credit card processing fees (2.9%+) add unnecessary cost to your unit price. T/T is flat-rate and cheaper for you.
2.  **Export Compliance:** Large payments must go directly to our **Manufacturing Entity (Suzhou V5 Medical Technology Co., Ltd.)** in China. This ensures we can issue valid VAT invoices for tax rebates, keeping your FOB prices competitive.
3.  **Anti-Money Laundering (AML):** Bank wires provide a clear audit trail required by customs in both the UK/EU and China.
