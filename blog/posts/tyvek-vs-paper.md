# Tyvek vs Medical Paper: When Buyers Choose Wrong

> **Category:** Medical Packaging  
> **Insight:** Packaging is a Regulatory Decision, not just a Price Decision.

## Overview
Choosing packaging is about:
1.  Sterilization compatibility
2.  Shelf life requirements
3.  Audit defensibility

## 1. When Tyvek Is Mandatory
You **must** consider Tyvek if:
* You use **EO (Ethylene Oxide)** or **Plasma** sterilization.
* You require a long shelf life (5 years+).
* The device is moisture-sensitive.
* High MDR scrutiny is expected (Tyvek's microbial barrier is superior).

## 2. When Medical Paper Is Enough
Medical grade paper is acceptable if:
* You use **Steam** sterilization.
* The device is low-risk.
* The market is highly cost-sensitive and shelf-life demands are lower.

## 3. Common Buyer Mistakes
* **Using paper for EO:** While possible, it often leads to validation issues.
* **No seal validation:** Auditors check seal strength (tensile/burst) rigorously.
* **Ignoring burst strength:** Cheap paper tears during transit, rendering the device non-sterile.
