# MOQ vs Unit Price: The Real Cost Curve

> **Category:** Supply Chain Strategy  
> **Target:** Procurement Managers

## The Buyer’s Illusion
Many buyers focus purely on **Unit Price**. Professionals focus on the **Total Cost Curve**.

## 1. Why MOQ Exists (And Always Will)
MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) covers:
* Setup & Validation time
* Regulatory overhead
* Scrap risk
* Labor efficiency

**Fact:** Low MOQ often leads to *higher* hidden costs.

## 2. The Hidden Cost Curve

| Factor | Low MOQ Scenario | Proper MOQ Scenario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unit Price** | Low (often fake/bait) | Stable / Optimized |
| **Quality Risk** | **High** | Low |
| **Lead Time** | Unpredictable | Controlled |
| **Supplier Priority** | Low | **High** |

## 3. Smart Buyers Negotiate Curves, Not Prices
Instead of fighting for a lower MOQ, winning buyers use:
* Annual volume commitments
* Tiered pricing structures
* Tooling amortization plans
* Forecast sharing

## 4. The Supplier You Want Says "No" First
Suppliers who accept *any* MOQ usually cut corners, shift costs to quality, or fail audits later. **A professional partner defines their limits.**
