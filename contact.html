Frequently Asked Questions

What is your general MOQ?

For sutures, MOQ is typically 500 dozens per spec. For standard packs, 5,000 pcs. We support mixed-container orders.

Do you provide samples?

Yes. Standard samples are free; shipping cost is collected (FedEx/DHL). Custom OEM samples may incur tooling fees.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms are 30% Deposit, 70% Balance before shipment. We accept T/T, L/C, and Credit Card via Stripe for samples.
